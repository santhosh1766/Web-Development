Ajith Kumar

Indian Actor | Racer | Role Model

About

Ajith Kumar is a leading Indian film actor in Tamil cinema. He is known for his simplicity, discipline, and dedication to his craft. Apart from acting, he has a strong passion for motorsports and aviation.

Career Highlights

  • Started acting career in the early 1990s
  • Popular films include Mankatha, Billa, Vedalam, Viswasam
  • Won multiple Filmfare & State Awards
  • Known for avoiding media publicity
